# Financial Forecasting

Event-driven and market prediction engines.

## Overview

Financial Forecasting applies online learning and structural model discovery to event-driven market prediction, macroeconomic releases, and asset price signals under noisy conditions.

## Key Capabilities

- **Event-Driven Signals**: Real-time parsing of market events, macro data releases, and earnings shocks.
- **Online Adaptation**: Continuous model recalibration to adapt to regime shifts without offline retraining.
- **Uncertainty Quantification**: Robust confidence bounds around directional forecasts.
