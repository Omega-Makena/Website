Scarcity

Scarcity is an online relationship-discovery engine for systems that drift under scarce data — it learns the typed structure, the form, of a dynamic system from thin, noisy streams. That central finding — form survives when magnitudes do not — grew into a theory of its own, Organizational Identity Theory. The work below spans the engine, the theory it produced, and the national-scale platforms built on it.

Theory

Organizational Identity Theory

The foundational paper. What can be recovered from systems that drift under scarcity — and why form survives when magnitudes do not. Evidence from macroeconomics, biology, and financial markets.
